# The refinement walk keeps the run-level F (2026-11-06)

Branch `fixed-f`, from main 85e5ecc9. Spec changed: `neighbour_evidence_pass.md` §3.5.

## 1. What was wrong

The refinement pass walks each sample's two copies along the window. Besides copying two templates,
the walk has an autozygous state — both copies copying one template — entered with a long-run
share `F`. `F` started at the sample's inbreeding coefficient from the parameters file (fitted over
the whole run) and was then re-estimated, on the first windows of the run, as the mean probability
over the sites walked that the sample is autozygous.

In a 40-sample outbred cohort with every sample at 2× (msprime, 4 Mb, inbreeding 0), the pass made
genotypes worse than calling alone did:

| true genotype | right, refinement off | right, on (main) | wrong, off | wrong, on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| heterozygous | 7,521 | 3,344 | 4,427 | 8,792 |
| homozygous alternative | 6,664 | 8,163 | 936 | 49 |
| homozygous reference | 15,196 | 16,981 | 531 | 144 |

It called heterozygotes homozygous. A diagnostic build (branch `fit-check-diag`, not merged) logged
the walk's `F` per sample: **0.83 on average, up to 0.99**, against 0.05 to 0.07 for the samples of
every depth in the cohorts with deep samples, and against the run-level estimate of 0 to 0.4 for
these same samples. When every template is a 2× sample, one soft template explains both of a
sample's copies about as well as two, nothing holds the autozygous share down, and each window's
re-estimate fed the next.

## 2. The change

| what | where | spec |
|---|---|---|
| The walk's `F` is the parameters file's, clamped to [0.01, 0.99], for the whole run; it is no longer re-estimated from the walk. `S` and `R` are fitted as before | `SampleFit` in `fit.rs` (the `autozygous_probability_sum` and `autozygous_sites` fields are gone) | pass §3.5 |

## 3. How it was chosen

Three ways were measured on the all-2× cohort, heterozygotes right of 13,709:

| the walk's `F` | heterozygotes right | homozygotes wrong |
|---|---|---|
| re-estimated (main) | 3,344 | 193 |
| the parameters file's, kept | 5,007 | 367 |
| 0.01, kept (the bound) | 5,161 | 416 |

The run-level estimate is itself inflated at 2× (0 to 0.4 for an outbred cohort), but walking at
the floor gains only 154 more heterozygotes: the re-estimate was the defect, not the starting value.

**A run without a fitted `F`** — the parameters file's default is 0 — loses the re-estimate's rescue
of an inbred cohort. Measured on the 40-sample selfing cohort (inbreeding 0.9, 30× to 2×) walked
from `F` 0.01: kept there, every genotype class moved by at most 7 against the fitted `F`
re-estimated. The walk sees autozygosity through its templates — a sample whose two copies match
one template is called homozygous whatever `F` says.

## 5. What is left

- **The all-2× cohort still loses heterozygotes to refinement**: 5,007 right against 7,521 with it
  off, though it gains more homozygotes than that (all three classes: 29,597 right against 29,381).
  Traced to the rounds, not to calling's lean against heterozygotes: with every sample's `F` set to
  0 in the parameters file, refinement still lost them (8,051 → 5,159). By the number of rounds,
  genotypes averaged over rounds 2 to the last, `F` kept:

  | rounds | heterozygotes right | homozygous alternative wrong | homozygous reference wrong |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 1,749 | 51 | 72 |
  | 2 | 7,111 | 458 | 833 |
  | 3 | 4,636 | 120 | 288 |
  | 5 (the pass's) | 5,007 | 102 | 265 |
  | refinement off | 7,521 | 936 | 531 |

  Round 1 copies other samples' genotypes, and a copy of a heterozygous template predicts either
  allele at even odds, so it calls few heterozygotes; it only seeds the rounds. Round 2 copies
  haplotypes drawn from round 1 and restores them. Rounds 3 to 5 drift back: each round's haplotypes
  are drawn from the round before, and with no deep sample to hold them the panel loses
  heterozygosity. Where deep samples are present the later rounds help in every class — on the
  30×/10×/5×/2× outbred cohort, round 5 against round 2 at 5×: heterozygotes wrong 200 → 146,
  references wrong 91 → 46 — so fewer rounds is no fix. A fix has to detect the drift, for example
  by the cohort's called heterozygosity against what its allele frequencies and `F` expect. Not
  built.
- The run-level `F` is inflated at 2× (0 to 0.4 where the truth is 0); walking at the floor instead
  gains 154 heterozygotes in 13,709 there. Not changed.

## 6. Tests

- `fit::tests::the_autozygous_share_is_the_run_level_one` replaces
  `the_autozygous_share_follows_the_sample`: on a drawn cohort of 20 samples at 2 reads a record,
  inbred and outbred, fitting a window leaves `F` where the parameters file put it.
- The two bit-fingerprint tests of a phased window (`refine::tests`) re-recorded; the test window's
  phased columns fall from 2,871 to 2,782.
- The whole crate's tests pass in release, except the two `should_panic` tests outside the pass
  (`alignment::left_align_structured`, `genetics::lgamma`) that panic only through debug
  assertions, which a release build leaves out.

## 7. Reproducing

The 40-sample cohorts are built with
`doc/devel/ng/research/neighbour_evidence/refine_sources/natural_cohort.sh` (`DEPTHS=2:40` and
inbreeding 0 for the all-2× cohort), called with `call-from-psps --refine-genotypes on` (and `off`
for the comparison), and scored with `benchmarks/suite/lib/refine_score.py` for phase and a
by-sample genotype count against the truth. The diagnostic build's switches: `PVC_DIAG_FIX_F` keeps
`F`, `PVC_DIAG_F_VALUE` sets it, `PVC_DIAG_FIT_CHECK` logs each sample-window's walk parameters.

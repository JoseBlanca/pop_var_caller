feat(ng): the ordinary-site seed pins its diversity, and shrinks its shape

The SNP/indel prior's two concentrations are an expected allele frequency and a
total conviction in other clothes. The total is now solved from the run's own
fitted heterozygosity instead of taken from the search, so the seed reproduces
that measurement exactly at every panel size and every shape — where the
two-parameter fit lost 9.9% of it at 63 individuals on a tomato-like density and
18.6% on a human-like one. The expected frequency is blended in log space
between the neutral shape and the panel's own, at a weight that rises with the
panel and travels to the run's output. spec/ordinary_site_seed.md §3 and §4.

The diversity is now read on every path through project_spectrum_seed, which is
a change to that function's contract: a spectrum used to make it moot.

Three refusals, each a regime rather than a silence: no total reaches the
measured diversity at the blended shape; a heterozygosity above a half, which no
pair can imply; and a cohort that measured no variation at all.

TWO RULINGS REMAIN OPEN AND ARE THE OWNER'S, both recorded in the report's §8.
The ramp has no measured support — every arm puts the best half-weight panel
size at zero, and the shipped 0.25 is a hedge costing 2.7% on held-out drawn
cohorts and 26% at a real cohort's segregating share. And the sweep's own
control contradicts §4's premise: the panel's fitted shape is exact at one
individual and degrades as the panel grows, because the shape is read back out
of allele-count classes rather than off the density that produced them. The
recommendation on file is to read it off the density in closed form, which
retires the ramp rather than retuning it.
